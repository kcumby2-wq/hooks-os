Section 03 · Weakest pillar

Revenue Architecture: 39/100.

The pillar most limiting Margin's business heading into season is not the credential, the audience, or the ambition of the three-funnel design. It's that the product ladder everyone agrees on isn't priced, and nothing on it can be bought.

What's there

A real five-tier ladder (Free → STL Membership → STL Cohort → Prospect Edge Evaluation → Coached by Hooks), plus Trainer Blueprint as a sixth, high-ticket sibling product. One locked, live price point: $99/month for STL Membership. A self-financing thesis for the $30K platform build (break-even at 25 sustained members) that the existing audience size makes plausible, not aspirational.

What's missing

Three things. First, four of five ladder rungs carry a price range instead of a price — Cohort, Evaluation, CBH deposit, and Trainer Blueprint are all still “TBD” or “Margin's call” in the source documents. Second, the transactional infrastructure behind the ladder doesn't exist yet: Stripe products aren't provisioned, and three of six required price variables are explicitly flagged as blocking Phase 2 of the build. Third — the one that costs the most, right now — the live site has zero purchase flow. A parent who wants to pay Margin today has no path to do it at the URL they'd actually visit.

Why this pillar is the one to fix first

Brand Identity and Content & Credibility both score higher because the raw material — the credentials, the audience, the two-brand story — already exists in abundance. Revenue Architecture is different: even a perfect storefront and a perfect voice calibration produce nothing until there are real numbers behind the ladder. Pricing is the one gap that blocks every other pillar's payoff from converting into revenue.

What fixes it

The ladder lock. Detailed in Section 04.
