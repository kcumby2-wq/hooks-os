# YOUR MISSION

You are building a **cinematic, live, one-page interactive experience** that reveals the **Margin Hooks operating brain** to a single viewer: **Margin himself**. Margin is a 20-year wide-receiver coach and player-management operator — 26 NFL players developed, 160+ D1 commits, 28 HS All-Americans, 6 Five-Stars, 1 Pro Bowler on his ledger. His signed roster includes 6 NFL and 30+ Power 4 college athletes. He is the most credentialed trainer in Kyron Cumby's Trail of Joy federation.

Margin has been paying Kyron for TOJ's Trainer OS Tier 3 service — but he has not yet **seen the brain** that runs behind it. He knows the deliverables. He hasn't felt the operating discipline.

Your job is to reveal it, and by revealing it: give Margin his Sunday nights back. Show him that his voice stays his own. Show him that his athletes are protected. Show him that his 20 years of coaching wisdom is compounding into a Personal LLM he owns.

You have ONE viewer. You have ~7 minutes. Every second earns its place.

# WHAT YOU ARE VISUALIZING

**hooks-os** is a GitHub repository at [github.com/kcumby2-wq/hooks-os](https://github.com/kcumby2-wq/hooks-os). It is not a traditional application. It is Margin's **operating brain** — a structured collection of 13 skill files, 2 workflows, data folders, configuration, and governance rules that instruct AI agents on how to run every operational surface of the Margin Hooks / Coached by Hooks / Sky's The Limit WR business.

Think of it this way:
- If Margin is the coach, hooks-os is the assistant coach
- If Margin is the practice, hooks-os is the film room
- If Margin is the voice, hooks-os is the amplifier

The brain runs the operations Margin doesn't have time for during season. Sunday-night parent emails. Monday morning briefs. Wednesday NIL follow-ups. Athlete outreach drafts. Advocacy call attribution. Content cadence monitoring. Every one of those has a skill file and, in some cases, an agent that executes them at Margin's approval.

Margin knows he pays for this service. He does not yet realize that a **12-month arc converts his 20 years of coaching wisdom into a Personal LLM he owns as IP** — productizable like his own Trainer Blueprint has been ($3,500–$5,000 per license).

You are showing him the brain. And you are showing him what compounds.

# AESTHETIC CONTRACT (non-negotiable)

**Brand palette:**
- **Background:** near-black `#0B0B0F` (slightly cooler than Pylon black — this is a trainer's world, not a partnership boardroom)
- **Primary accent:** warm gold `#E4B95E` (Margin's coaching-brand tone — earned, not electric)
- **Secondary accent:** deep field-green `#1E3A2C` used sparingly for "grown" or "compounding" moments (Personal LLM path)
- **Text:** warm off-white `#F5F1E8` for body · `#E4B95E` for emphasis
- **Divider/muted:** `#2B2B33`
- **Never use:** blue, red, purple, gradients, drop shadows heavier than `filter: drop-shadow(0 0 6px #E4B95E33)`

**Typography:**
- **Display:** Anton (dramatic, uppercase — Margin's brand is credential-forward)
- **Body:** Inter (weight 400 for body, weight 600 for callouts)
- **Data / eyebrow labels:** JetBrains Mono
- Fallback: `system-ui, "Segoe UI Black", Impact` for Anton; `system-ui, sans-serif` for Inter; `"SF Mono", Consolas, monospace` for JetBrains

**Motion language:**
- Motion is meaning. Every reveal earns its beat.
- Prefer easing curve `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)`
- Slow reveal over rapid transition
- Never bounce, flash, spin, or wiggle
- Reveals should feel like an athlete easing into a route, not a click-baited notification

**Sound (optional):**
- Optional low ambient tone for Act 2 awakening. Never stock sounds. Silent + typography-forward beats tacky every time.

**What the design is NOT:**
- Not a CRM dashboard
- Not a fitness-app UI (no big rings, no progress meters, no leaderboards)
- Not a marketing landing page
- Not a Zapier-style automation diagram with rectangular boxes and arrows

**Design references (study before writing code):**
- Nike's ad-film aesthetic — reverent, unhurried, athletic
- The final scene of *Chariots of Fire* — patience, weight, breath
- Nolan's *Interstellar* mission-control HUDs — utilitarian but earned
- Emil Kowalski's motion work (https://emilkowal.ski) — restraint as beauty
- Vercel documentation aesthetic — dense, dark, breathing room
- The opening minute of an Under Armour brand film — legacy over hype

# ANTI-PATTERNS · DO NOT DO

1. **Do not build a CRM dashboard.** No pipeline kanbans, no lead cards, no filter bars.
2. **Do not use fitness-app tropes.** No progress rings, no leaderboards, no streaks.
3. **Do not use emoji, clip art, or stock icons.** Every visual is type, geometry, or actual data.
4. **Do not fabricate data.** Every number in the demo is real (or clearly labeled projection).
5. **Do not use gradients.** Solid color only.
6. **Do not use hype vocabulary.** No "elevate," "unlock," "revolutionize," "game-changing." Read `margin-voice/SKILL.md` and hold that register.
7. **Do not include marketing CTAs.** No "Book a demo." No "Get started." This isn't landing-page copy — it's an operating-brain reveal.
8. **Do not overload sound.** Silence is fine. If sound is used, one ambient tone only.
9. **Do not use loading spinners.** Text-based countdown or nothing.
10. **Do not narrate the workflow in prose.** Show it moving. If you must caption, 5–8 words max.
11. **Do not use military / warfare framing.** ("Deploy tactics," "battle plans" etc.) Margin is a coach. This is training, not war.
12. **Do not name athletes by real full name.** Use position + school abbreviation only (WR · Coppell). Consent framework matters even in a demo.
