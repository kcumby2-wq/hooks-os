## 1. What changed

In October 2026 Ground Floor Sports (GFS) moved its marketplace from Flippa Highlights to
**SportsClippers**. The edits and digital assets made for the Coached by Hooks / STL roster
are listed and sold on SportsClippers, through GFS's storefront there.

## 2. Who does what

| | SportsClippers | GFS |
|---|---|---|
| **Does** | Hosts listings, checkout, licensing, delivery, payouts and buyer support | Films, tags, lists and prices the footage; runs the storefront; manages and pays its creators |
| **Owns** | Its platform and software | The footage it captures, except what the STL agreement leaves with STL |
| **Pays** | GFS, for every sale | STL its share, and GFS's own creators |

GFS sets each price within SportsClippers' license tiers, so the listing on SportsClippers is
the source of truth for what an edit or asset costs.

## 3. Where the money goes, per sale

| Of every $100 of net sales | |
|---|---:|
| SportsClippers' marketplace fee, kept at checkout | $25.00 |
| Edit reserve (10% of what GFS receives), spent on producing the media sold | $7.50 |
| **STL** — two-thirds of the rest | **$45.00** |
| **GFS** — one-third of the rest | **$22.50** |

*Net sales are the price less taxes, refunds and chargebacks. SportsClippers pays GFS
`[weekly]` with a statement for each sale, and GFS remits STL's share (STL agreement, SOW
§6(c)). On $10,000 of net sales, STL receives $4,500 and GFS $2,250, the same dollars as the
STL agreement's earlier split. Only the edit reserve is smaller, because SportsClippers
takes its 25% before GFS is paid.*

## 4. Creators

- Creators upload as members of GFS's storefront. SportsClippers pays GFS, and GFS pays them.
- A **freelance** creator gets a daily rate and no share of sales.
- A creator **signed to Hooks** is paid from STL's share, on the split in the
  [creator content agreement](../contracts/creator-content-agreement.md).

## 5. Minors and consent

SportsClippers applies its minor-protection standards (Schedule A of GFS's agreement) to every
GFS listing, on top of the `c4` guardian-consent gate:

- No athlete under 18 appears in a listing unless GFS has sent a guardian-consent flag.
- A listing that shows a minor is reachable only by private family link, unless the guardian
  chose public display. Under 13 is never listed publicly.
- No hometown, school schedule, measurements, contact details or location tags for a minor.
- No messaging between buyers or creators and a minor on the platform.
- Commercial licenses of footage showing a minor stay off unless the guardian gave commercial
  consent.
- Licensed music only. No AI training on GFS footage or athlete data, and no synthetic
  likenesses.
- Removal within 72 hours of a guardian's withdrawal or GFS's notice, from every page, search
  result and cache SportsClippers controls.

## 7. Handling

Every asset that features an athlete runs behind the guardian-consent gate and Schedule A. No
minor's likeness is listed, sold or downloaded without `c4` consent and counsel's NIL
clearance. Examples are illustrative; no real athlete data here.
