### 1 · The Operating Brain — *what runs the business*
The [`hooks-agents` console](https://www.tojcampaign.com/admin/hooks-agents.html) and
the `hooks-os` repo: lead capture + segmentation, consult booking + no-show recovery,
onboarding lifecycles, 30/14/7-day renewal cadence, 4–6h recap SLA, account-health
monitoring, and auto revenue-share tracking. **This is the part that means the work
happens whether or not you do it.** You coach; the brain runs the rest.

### 2 · The Recruiting Funnel — *the front door, going live*
`skysthelimit-site-v3.html` is a complete, finished recruiting site — the stakes-first
hero, the Instant-Win diagnostic, the product ladder (CBH · Prospect Edge · Cohort ·
Membership · Trainer Blueprint), the film room, the intake. **The front end is done.**
What's new: we're installing the backend (Supabase + GHL + Stripe) so it actually
captures the lead, gates the film, and takes the payment — with the minor-consent
rules enforced at the database, not just on the page. The developer handoff is written
and ready; this offer greenlights the build.

### 3 · The Diagnostic Engine — *how it keeps getting better*
The [AI Tools Assessment](https://ai-tools-assessment-hazel.vercel.app/) is the method
that *built* your operating brain: find the pain, automate it, measure the hours back.
Two roles going forward:
- **Internal:** it keeps running on your operation — every quarter it finds the next
  thing to automate, so the machine compounds instead of going stale.
- **External:** it's a packaged product your **Trainer Blueprint** network can resell
  to their own businesses — a new revenue line that rides on the certification you
  already sell.

### 4 · The Digital Asset Layer — *Flippa Highlights (new)*
**[Flippa Highlights](https://www.flippahighlights.com/marketplace) is the Digital
Asset Partner.** Today a highlight is content that gets watched once and forgotten.
Through Flippa Highlights, an athlete's highlights become **digital assets** —
distributed and monetized through a [marketplace](https://www.flippahighlights.com/marketplace)
and [download](https://www.flippahighlights.com/download), not just posted.

What this adds to the machine:
- **A new surface for every highlight** Subject Media already produces — the same
  capture now has a monetization path, not just a feed.
- **A reason the content compounds** — assets that live in a marketplace keep working
  after the post scrolls away.
- **A cleaner offer to families** — "we don't just film him, we turn it into something
  he owns and can distribute."

*(Consent gate applies: for minors, the highlight only becomes a public/tradable asset
with guardian consent — same `c3`/`c4` rules the recruiting funnel enforces. Flippa
Highlights sits behind that gate, never around it.)*

### 5 · The Commercial Frame — *the deal*
The [Master Operating Agreement](../contracts/toj-margin-master-operating-agreement.md)
governs it: **TOJ receives 10% of Collected Revenue on roster referrals** for as long
as they're active, auto-calculated from the ledger — no manual reconciliation. You own
the coaching craft, the brand, the relationships, and the athlete data; TOJ owns the
operating-brain methodology. Clean boundary, on paper.

## How they interlock — the flywheel

```
Recruiting Funnel captures a lead   (2)
   → Operating Brain works it to a paying client, tracked   (1)
      → Subject Media films the athlete
         → Flippa Highlights turns the highlight into an owned, monetizable asset   (4)
            → the asset + the results become the funnel's next proof   (→ 2)
   ↑ Diagnostic Engine keeps finding the next hour to automate   (3)
   ↑ Commercial Frame tracks + shares the revenue the whole loop produces   (5)
```

Each piece feeds the next. That's the difference between five tools and one operating
system: the machine gets more valuable every turn, and the same coaching authority
powers all of it.
